Our membership has concluded its latest quarterly audit of funded members as of November 20, 2026\. This, our 18th consecutive update reaffirms our institution's consistency and ability to self-regulate.

The member registry now includes a newly eligible Formal Verification team, a number of new contributor accounts, and a larger than usual cohort of new alumni; creating a representative body of the developers and maintainers who work on client implementations, research, and upgrade delivery.

Protocol Guild's membership is now 172, with a net decrease of 18 (-9.5%) members from 190 of last quarter. This quarter, the membership update was once again processed onchain via Protocol Guild's Agora-based DAO contracts. Readers will find a comprehensive list of the changeset below.
