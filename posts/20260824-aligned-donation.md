Aligned has fulfilled its Protocol Guild Pledge, completing its donation of 1.5% of the $ALIGN token supply to Ethereum's core contributors.

When Aligned made their pledge in December 2024, they didn't just match the [recommended 1% token allocation](https://www.protocolguild.org/blog/20240130-protocol-guild-pledge) - they went one step further by pledging 1.5% of their entire token supply. That extra half-percent speaks to Aligned's recognition that their infrastructure wouldn't exist without the years of painstaking work by Ethereum's protocol researchers, client developers, and cryptographers. To quote their pledge announcement: "We wouldn't be here without Ethereum and its core developers. Ethereum is the world computer and one of the most important innovations of our time."

**Building a Full Stack on Ethereum**

Aligned is building a full stack that lets fintechs, institutions, and enterprises build financial products on Ethereum: wallets, rollups, interoperability, and proof aggregation in a single integration, rather than assembling separate vendors for each piece.

One core part of that stack is the Proof Aggregation Service, which recursively compresses many proofs into a single proof verified on Ethereum, enabling full Ethereum security without the prohibitive gas costs. Rather than verifying every proof directly on L1, where costs climb as more rollups and applications launch, Aligned batches them together, so verification stays economical at scale.

The rest of the stack builds on the same principle of removing friction for developers: Wallet-as-a-Service (non-custodial wallets with no seed phrases and no browser extensions), Rollup-as-a-Service (based rollups powered by @ethrex_client), LambdaVM (a RISC-V zkVM, or zero-knowledge virtual machine, built in collaboration with LambdaClass and 3MI Labs), and an interoperability layer connecting Aligned-powered rollups with the broader Ethereum ecosystem.

All of it ultimately settles on Ethereum's base layer, depending on the same core infrastructure that Protocol Guild contributors maintain and upgrade: consensus, execution, and cryptography.

**Why This Matters Now**

Ethereum has proven itself with the highest security guarantees and zero downtime. The remaining challenge is bringing the next billion users onchain - without sacrificing the decentralization that makes Ethereum valuable in the first place.

Projects like Aligned are tackling this by building a full stack that makes launching real financial products on Ethereum practical. But none of it works without the base layer running smoothly beneath it.

The 1% Pledge exists to make this interdependence explicit and sustainable. Aligned's donation will vest over four years via Protocol Guild's [immutable vesting contract](https://explorer.splits.org/accounts/0x25941dc771bb64514fc8abbce970307fb9d477e9/), which today supports 190 of Ethereum's active core protocol contributors. This represents alignment in the most literal sense: ensuring that the people building on Ethereum and the people building Ethereum itself share in the value they collectively create.
