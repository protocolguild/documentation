A new fund dedicated to core development coordination and development. Our first opinionated allocation will be to fund ACDE coordination in support of Nixo's work, in partnership with [EthCoordinate](https://ethcoordinate.org/) (fka Ethstaker).

**Context**

Protocol Guild's need-blind allocation was designed for a world of abundant token capital speculation. The theory, which played out in part, was that Protocol Guild could be a managed vehicle for core dev exposure to these broader economic dynamics. That world has largely disappeared. The number of new, speculative streams which we can surface funding flows and ecosystem exposure from have dropped. Today we are confronted with the new realities:

- capital scarcity
- teams needing to make payroll, and plan for potential budget reductions. This is the reality that PG allocation strategies need to adapt to, or otherwise risk quickly becoming irrelevant.
- significant [personnel and budget cuts](https://blog.ethereum.org/2026/06/23/ef-structure) at the EF

**Funding ACD Coordination**

In recognition of these challenges, PGF and Ethcoordinate (fka EthStaker) will be collaborating to fund Nixo's ACD coordination role directly. This is a critical role in core development:

- requires the right temperament: firm when managing a call but able to work people towards consensus in cases of disagreement
- ideally should not be a person on one of the client teams
- requires attention to detail
- requires high context on the state of EIPs and their tradeoffs
- requires meaningful relationships/engagement within the upgrade process

Her role carries the same accountability expectations as other PGF-funded roles: a role description, quarterly reports and annual reviews, and oversight from the broader membership. This is a direct staffing decision within PGF's existing operating budget. Other unfunded coordination or research roles will be considered separately, on their own merits.

**Conclusion**

PGF and EthCoordinate funding this work fills a critical gap, and allows planning for Glamsterdam and Hegota to continue.
