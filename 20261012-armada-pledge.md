Protocol Guild is proud to announce that [Armada](https://armada.blue/) has taken the [1% Pledge](https://www.protocolguild.org/blog/20240130-protocol-guild-pledge) - committing 1% of the ARM token supply to support Ethereum's core contributors. What makes this pledge distinctive is its structure: like Armada's own tokenomics, the donation will be revenue-locked, unlocking only as the protocol achieves real usage milestones.

**Privacy That Disappears**

Armada is building plug-in privacy infrastructure for stablecoins. Their thesis is simple: as stablecoin settlement grows - from payroll to vendor payments to treasury management - organizations cannot expose their entire financial graph onchain. But neither will they adopt crypto-native privacy apps. Instead, privacy must disappear into the custody, treasury, and payment products they already trust. To quote Armada, “the winning privacy layer is the one users never realize they used”.

Most privacy protocols deploy a separate pool per chain. Armada's approach concentrates liquidity rather than fragmenting it, via a hub-and-spoke architecture. Ethereum serves as the hub, where a single shielded pool holds all private state. Other chains connect as spokes. When a user shields USDC on Base, Circle's CCTP burns it there and mints it on Ethereum into the shared pool. When spent, the transaction reveals nothing about which chain it came from. Every new spoke adds its users to the same pool, making the privacy stronger for everyone.

This design reflects a core belief: privacy compounds with concentration. A fragmented ecosystem of specialized privacy products weakens everyone. Armada's shielded pool is designed as neutral infrastructure - no company or governing entity can take control of it.

**Adoption-Based Donation**

Armada's Protocol Guild pledge mirrors their own token design. The ARM token has no VC allocation and no private allocations. Core contributor, advisor, and airdropped tokens are revenue-locked: they carry no control rights until the protocol earns revenue. Until then, crowdfund participants govern the treasury and fee policy.

Armada's 1% Protocol Guild donation follows the same principle. Rather than transferring tokens immediately, the donation will be distributed based on protocol usage milestones - ensuring that support for Ethereum core development scales alongside Armada's actual adoption. This creates a natural alignment: as Armada grows, so does its contribution to the infrastructure it depends on.

**Why This Matters**

Privacy infrastructure is essential infrastructure. As the global economy moves onchain, the ability to conduct routine financial activity without exposing sensitive information will determine how much of that activity Ethereum captures. Armada is betting that the winning privacy layer will be infrastructure, not an app - and that it must be durable enough for serious organizations to build on.

That durability depends on Ethereum's base layer continuing to upgrade and scale. Armada's hub-and-spoke architecture relies on Ethereum as the central settlement layer. Every cross-chain shielded transfer ultimately settles on Ethereum, secured by the consensus and cryptography that Protocol Guild contributors maintain.

By taking the 1% Pledge, Armada is ensuring that the value flowing through its privacy layer flows back to the people making that layer possible. The revenue-locked structure reinforces a shared commitment: sustainable funding for sustainable infrastructure.
